Demo mode

A simulated bridge with a week of history, for trying things out. It starts on its own when nothing is set up.

Vibration is measured by three accelerometer nodes at a quarter, half and three quarters of the span. Scores come from an autoencoder trained on this bridge's normal behaviour.
